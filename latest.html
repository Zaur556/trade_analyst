How the model works

Transparent, rule-based, no black box

Five factors

Trend (EMA 20/50/200, ADX), momentum (RSI, MACD, 20-day return, 52-week position), mean reversion (Bollinger, distance from EMA, RSI extremes), news flow (event rules over 96 hours) and macro (dollar, yields, VIX, S&P, copper). Each factor is squashed to [-1, +1].

Per-instrument weights

Brent leans on trend, momentum and geopolitics. Gold and silver weight macro heavily. Natural gas runs in fade mode: its trend and momentum readings are inverted because gas mean-reverts, and news/weather carry more weight.

Decision rule

Weighted score in [-100, +100]. ≥ +45 strong buy, ≥ +20 buy, between -20 and +20 hold, ≤ -20 sell, ≤ -45 strong sell. Confidence blends factor agreement, data quality, news coverage and trend clarity.

Trade construction

Stop at roughly 1.6x ATR (capped at 2.2x, widened to the 10-day swing only when it is close). Target at 4x ATR, giving about 1.8:1 to 2.2:1. Position size is derived from a fixed fraction of account equity, not from conviction.

Data

Daily OHLCV from Yahoo Finance continuous front-month futures. Headlines from 13 curated RSS feeds plus 9 Google News searches. The last daily bar can be an intraday snapshot; prices are indicative, not executable.

Known limits

No weather model, no storage balance, no CFTC positioning, no options skew, no intraday microstructure. News scoring is lexical and rule-based, so irony and novel events can be misread. Geopolitical premium can vanish in one session.

Risk notice. This tool produces a systematic, rules-based opinion from public data. It is not investment advice, a solicitation, or a guarantee. Commodity futures are leveraged: a move smaller than the stop can still produce losses larger than the margin posted, and gaps can jump the stop. Headline sentiment is approximate, prices may be delayed or wrong, and the model has no knowledge of your portfolio, taxes, horizon or risk capacity. Verify every price with your broker and size positions so that being wrong is survivable.
